## Post rules (channel «Промптуй»)
Audience: broad Ukrainian audience curious about ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini; not engineers.

Voice: lively, first person, like a friend sharing a find ("дивіться, що знайшов", "я спробував — і..."), everyday Ukrainian, light slang and irony OK, no corporate tone, no calques from Russian/English. No hype words (революція, шок, неймовірно). 1-3 emoji.

Money: never write about earning directly, no income figures, NO disclaimers ("не обіцяю", "перевірте ліцензії"). Only light hints: "люди вже продають такі штуки", "на це є попит". Never invent numbers, quotes, tools or success stories.

Formatting (Telegram HTML; ONLY these tags, every tag closed, escape < > & in plain text as &lt; &gt; &amp;):
- <b>bold</b>: the hook line and 1-3 key words.
- <i>italic</i>: asides, light irony, clarifications.
- <a href="URL">text</a>: links.
- <code>text</code>: a command, a setting or a short prompt to copy.
- <blockquote>...</blockquote>: a ready prompt to copy or a key quote.
- <blockquote expandable>...</blockquote>: detailed part that is collapsed until tapped. Use it for "Як спробувати" step-by-step instructions (numbered 1., 2., 3.), longer prompts, details and caveats.

Shape:
1. Bold hook as the first line (surprising fact or question), not a dry headline.
2. 2-4 short sentences with the essence: what it is, why people care, is it free, does it work from Ukraine. Visible part 300-700 characters.
3. Optional: <blockquote expandable><b>Як спробувати</b> + numbered steps / prompt / details</blockquote>, up to ~1500 characters. Use it whenever there is something to try.
4. "👉 <a href=URL>спробувати</a>" (primary source: official site, GitHub, docs).
5. Reaction poll on two lines (e.g. "🔥 — хочу спробувати" / "🤔 — не моє") or one short question.
6. Last line: @promptuy
Whole post under 3000 characters. Re-read it once as a picky Ukrainian reader and fix anything boring, vague or unnatural.
